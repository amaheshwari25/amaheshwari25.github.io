I’m an incoming PhD student in MIT EECS, affiliated with CSAIL and LIDS, where I’m lucky to be advised by Costis Daskalakis and Manish Raghavan. I am broadly interested in the intersections of theoretical computer science, machine learning, and game theory.

I completed my undergraduate studies in computer science, with a minor in math, at Princeton, where I had the fortune of being advised by Matt Weinberg. Before starting my PhD, I spent a year at Carnegie Mellon working with Elaine Shi. I’m grateful to be supported by an NSF Graduate Research Fellowship, and to have received the Phillip Goldman ’86 Senior Prize in Computer Science (top CS student at Princeton) and a Goldwater Scholarship as an undergraduate.
