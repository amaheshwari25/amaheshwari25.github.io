I’m an incoming PhD student in MIT EECS, affiliated with CSAIL and LIDS, where I’m fortunate to be advised by Costis Daskalakis and Manish Raghavan. I am broadly interested in theoretical computer science, machine learning, game theory, and their intersections.

I completed my undergraduate studies in computer science, with a minor in mathematics, at Princeton, where I was advised by Matt Weinberg. Before beginning my PhD, I spent a year at Carnegie Mellon working with Elaine Shi. I’m grateful to be supported by an NSF Graduate Research Fellowship. As an undergraduate, I received the Phillip Goldman ’86 Senior Prize in Computer Science—Princeton CS’s top academic honor—and a Goldwater Scholarship.
